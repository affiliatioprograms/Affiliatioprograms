How does AffiliatePrograms rank platforms?

Each platform is scored on five weighted pillars: Trust & Security (30%), Pricing & Value (25%), Usability (20%), Support (15%) and User Consensus (10%).

Do I have to pay to be listed?

No. Directory placement cannot be purchased. Rankings come from live testing, published pricing and aggregated user reviews.

Are the platforms safe to use?

Yes — provided the platform is transparent about its terms. We verify licences where applicable and flag any platform with an unresolved dispute.

How often is this directory updated?

Every month. Scores are recalculated, new platforms are added, and any platform that fails our criteria is removed immediately.
